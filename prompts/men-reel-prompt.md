# Reusable prompt: The Strong Man Code reel (men's page)

Copy everything inside the box, fill in the TOPIC, SCRIPT and colour lines, and send it.
In this kit, Claude loads the `strong-man-reel` skill automatically. The prompt also works on its own.

```text
Act like a senior motion designer and Remotion engineer. Build a premium, words-only
motion-graphics reel: no people, faces or footage, just kinetic typography plus the symbolic
objects described below. Use high-quality HyperFrames and hyper-motion throughout.

QUALITY BAR (hyper-motion, high-quality HyperFrames):
- Treat every frame as a designed frame: no static moments. Something always drifts, breathes or moves.
- Layered depth: background, mid and foreground layers moving at different speeds (parallax),
  slow camera push-ins, and camera moves that carry the eye from one scene to the next.
- Motion with intent: at least 3 different eases per scene, entrances that overlap, staggered letters
  and words, objects that anticipate and settle. Nothing linear, nothing robotic.
- Transitions are designed moments that carry meaning, never a default cut or plain crossfade.
- Every word lands exactly on its spoken timestamp, with sound effects locked to the same beats.
- Pixel-level polish: safe margins (90 px sides, 120 px top and bottom), no overflow, no overlapping
  text, crisp readable type at phone size, self-hosted fonts, a deterministic timeline.
- Render at high quality (HyperFrames --quality high, H.264, CRF 18 master) and check frames
  from the actual MP4, not only previews, before delivering.

Make a new reel for my men's page "The Strong Man Code" in the same structure as the approved
"The habit no man talks about" video.

TOPIC: [e.g. waking up early / saving money / leaving your phone / training when tired]
SCRIPT: [paste your script, or write "write it for me and show me before building"]
ACCENT COLOUR: [blood red (default) / ember orange / steel blue / brass gold / forest green]

THE STRUCTURE (keep all of this exactly):

Format
- Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, 25-35 seconds, built in HyperFrames (use the strong-man-reel skill).
- Faceless: no people, faces, bodies or photos. Words plus symbolic objects only. No emojis.

Script (55-80 words, US spelling, simple UK/US English)
- Voice of a wise, charismatic older man: calm, direct, certain, a little cold. Short heavy sentences
  spoken to "you". No jokes, slang, shouting, begging for likes, or putting down women or any group.
- Arc: hook (a hard truth or curiosity line) -> the weak man and what he does -> the turn ->
  the strong man's actions (3-4 very short lines) -> the rule -> CTA "Follow for more rules."
- Weak man first, strong man last. No medical or financial claims.
- Example of the register: "The habit no man talks about. A weak man gives in every time he is bored,
  tired or alone. Then he hates himself, and repeats it tomorrow. A strong man knows an urge is a wave.
  It rises. It passes. He stands up. He leaves the room. He moves his body. The phone stays outside the
  bedroom. Control your urges, or they will control you. Follow for more rules."

Voice and sound
- Deep, wise, masculine ElevenLabs narrator (approved voice: "Bill Adams", V2bPluzT7MuirpucVAKH),
  whole script in one take with pauses. Estimate the cost first. Fallback: the local Kokoro voice.
- Procedural sound design under the voice: whoosh on every cut, impacts and stamps on slams, a sub hit
  under the biggest weak-side line and under the rule, a low drone, all ducked under the voice.

Look
- Black canvas, ONE accent colour, warm bone-white text, desaturated grey for the weak side.
- Fonts: Big Shoulders Display 900 for statements (100-330 px), JetBrains Mono for labels and
  connector words. Film grain, breathing vignette, thin accent progress line at the bottom.
- NO text headers: no tags, no timers, no section labels at the top.

Story devices
- The two men are a symbol pair: a hollow grey outlined pawn (weak) and a solid, lit king (strong).
- Every spoken word appears on screen exactly as it is spoken (the words are the captions).
- One concrete object per scene, taken from the line's noun, and it ACTS on the spoken word
  (strings pull the pawn, a mirror cracks, a calendar flips and loops, a wave rises and passes,
  a door opens, the king hops across a chessboard, a phone slides out and a lock clicks).
- A different text layout in every scene, never the same centred vertical stack twice in a row:
  slam + typewriter, label + stamp, terminal line + icon row, broken type, word on an object,
  text on a path, editorial diagonal, speed type, hanging words, giant word + small line.

Transitions
- Primary (about 60%): a red blade slash that sweeps across while the scene swaps under it.
- Accents chosen for meaning: redaction flood (a secret exposed), glass crack (breaking point),
  tape rewind (a loop), page tear (a new day / topic change), zoom through a doorway (entering a
  new space), 4-frame dip to black (weight before the rule), blade cut (the strings cut free).
- No scene exits before its transition. The ending holds still.

Ending
- Hold on the rule, a small king emblem, "FOLLOW FOR MORE RULES" and the page name
  "THE STRONG MAN CODE", readable as a thumbnail.

Deliver
- Show me the script first (unless I gave it). Then build, check every scene and transition in
  stills and in the rendered MP4, fix any overlaps or unreadable text, and give me a high-quality
  MP4, a share copy under 30 MB and a thumbnail, with the scene timings.
```
