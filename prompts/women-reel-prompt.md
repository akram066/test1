# Base prompt: Her Calm Corner reel (women's page)

Copy everything inside the box, fill in the TOPIC and SCRIPT lines (AVOID is optional), and send it.
It keeps the structure and the motion quality of the page's reels, and asks for a brand-new creative
concept every time: a new motif, objects, palette and transitions.

```text
Act like a senior motion designer and Remotion engineer. Build a premium, words-only
motion-graphics reel: no people, faces or footage, just kinetic typography plus soft illustrated
objects. Use high-quality HyperFrames and hyper-motion throughout.

This is for my women's page "Her Calm Corner".

TOPIC: [e.g. rest without guilt / a simple glow routine / saying no kindly / a small savings habit]
SCRIPT: [paste your script, or write "write it for me and show me before building"]
AVOID: [optional: ideas used in recent reels that must not come back]

DO NOT COPY PAST REELS
- Invent a fresh creative concept for this reel. Do not reuse the motif, objects, palette, layouts
  or transitions of any earlier reel (including the starter reel inside the her-calm-reel skill and
  everything in video-projects/). Look at what already exists and make this one clearly different.
- Use the her-calm-reel skill only for its pipeline: voice, word timing, sound mix, build, lint,
  render and checks. Its starter reel is an example of quality, not a design to copy.

QUALITY BAR (hyper-motion, high-quality HyperFrames)
- Treat every frame as a designed frame: no static moments. Something always drifts, breathes or moves.
- Layered depth: background, mid and foreground layers moving at different speeds (parallax),
  slow camera push-ins, and camera moves that carry the eye from one scene to the next.
- Motion with intent: at least 3 different eases per scene, entrances that overlap, staggered letters
  and words, objects that wind up before they move and settle after. Nothing linear, nothing robotic.
- Transitions are designed moments that carry meaning, never a default cut or plain crossfade.
- Every word lands exactly on its spoken timestamp, with sound effects locked to the same beats.
- Pixel-level polish: safe margins (90 px sides, 120 px top and bottom), no overflow, no overlapping
  text, crisp readable type at phone size, self-hosted fonts, a deterministic timeline.
- Render at high quality (HyperFrames --quality high, H.264, CRF 18 master) and check frames
  from the actual MP4, not only previews, before delivering.

THE STRUCTURE (the same in every reel)

Format
- Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, about 28-36 seconds, built in HyperFrames.
- Faceless: no people, faces, bodies or photos. No emojis.

Script (50-80 words, US spelling, simple UK/US English, always original)
- Voice of a kind, calm older sister: gentle, supportive, honest, never judging, never rushing.
- It must sound SAID, not written: contractions (you're, don't, isn't), concrete specifics from her
  real life, "I know..." once, and steps with a believable size ("for a minute", "just one").
- Arc: a gentle opener + the kind thing nobody said -> name the feeling, then her real life in
  specifics -> take the pressure off -> one small step (or 2-3 tiny physical steps) -> a reframe
  people save -> a kind close or a gentle question -> a soft CTA.
- Validate first, then one small step. No shame, pressure, fear, comparing women, putting down men,
  or medical, diet or money claims.

Voice and sound
- A warm, calm female narrator that sounds like one real person talking: lines that belong together
  are spoken in one breath, the pauses vary, soft breaths before new phrases, a quiet room tone, a
  little slower on the steps and the ending, and one real pause on screen. ElevenLabs if available
  (estimate the cost first; warm and calm, not whispery or hyped), otherwise the local voice in
  breath-group mode.
- Soft sound design built for this concept (airy whooshes, chimes, gentle textures, a warm pad).
  Nothing hard: no impacts, slams or sub hits.

Look (new every reel, same mood)
- A light, warm, living background under every scene whose colour shifts with the mood of each
  scene, with drifting glows and soft texture. Choose a new palette for this topic (change it
  between reels); keep it soft and calm with one warm accent for the comforting words.
- An elegant serif with italic emphasis for statements and a friendly sans for small text; pick a
  pairing that suits the concept.
- Soft illustrated objects with gradients, highlights and gentle shadows (not flat line art).
- A life layer (grain or texture, a warm vignette, a thin progress line). No text headers.

Story devices (invent new ones every reel)
- ONE NEW recurring motif made from the topic's world that travels through the whole reel and turns
  into each scene's object, so the reel feels like one continuous shot (for example: a thread that
  becomes a path, a petal, a paper boat, a ribbon of light, a bird, a ripple, a key). Its
  transformations carry most transitions. Never the same motif twice in a row.
- Every spoken word appears on screen as it is spoken, floating in softly. The comforting word gets
  a special entrance and the payoff gets a hand-drawn accent.
- Objects act on their words (fill, open, bloom, settle, light up). Overwhelming things soften and
  leave (they drift, fade or blow away); they never break or shatter.
- A different text layout in every scene: words on objects, a giant italic word with small lines,
  a staircase of growing lines, text inside an object, an editorial headline with a payoff in the
  corner, a final card. Invent new ones too.
- Slow camera push-ins in every scene; a list of steps can be one world the camera travels through.

Transitions (invent new ones every reel)
- Most transitions are the motif transforming into the next scene's object (match cuts).
- 2-4 soft accents, each chosen for what that cut means (letting go, a new start, a breath, a calm
  reset before the close). Never a plain crossfade between unrelated scenes.

Ending
- Hold on the kind line, a callback to the motif, the CTA and the page name "HER CALM CORNER",
  readable as a thumbnail.

Deliver
- Before building, show me: the script (unless I gave it), a one-paragraph creative concept (the
  motif and what it becomes in each scene, the palette, the transitions and why they fit the topic)
  and the scene table (line -> motif / object -> text layout -> transition -> sound).
- Then build, check every scene and transition in stills and in the rendered MP4, fix any overlaps
  or unreadable text, and give me a high-quality MP4, a share copy under 30 MB and a thumbnail, with
  the scene timings.
```

## Add-on: image posts

Paste this part (instead of, or after, the reel prompt) when you want image posts. Attach your profile picture.

```text
IMAGE POSTS for my women's page "Her Calm Corner".

TOPIC: [the idea for the post or posts]
HOW MANY: [1 / 3 / 5]
FORMAT: [text card / then vs now / photo + headline / you choose the best fit]
MY PROFILE PICTURE: attached     HANDLE: [@yourhandle]

Act like a senior social media designer. Make each post an original 1080x1350 PNG (4:5), designed
by you (HTML/CSS rendered to an image, self-hosted fonts), and check it at phone size before you
give it to me.

Formats
1. Text card: looks like a native social post. Warm cream background (#F6EFE8) with a very soft
   blush glow, my profile picture as a perfect circle top-left, page name in bold cocoa (#3A2B2E)
   with my handle in a soft grey under it, then the post in a clean, friendly sans (cocoa, 40-46 px,
   left-aligned), short lines with a blank line between ideas. The one comforting phrase in rose
   italic serif (#AE5550). 8-14 short lines at most.
2. Then vs now: the same card style written as a gentle contrast, 3-5 rounds:
   WHAT I USED TO THINK: "..." / WHAT I KNOW NOW: "...", ending with one kind reminder.
   Labels in rose, the old thoughts in soft taupe, the new ones in cocoa.
3. Photo + headline: a soft, warm, natural-light photo fills the top 60-65% (a window, tea, a
   journal, flowers, a calm room, or hands and objects, never a face). Below it, on a cream band,
   a thin rose line with my small round profile picture and handle in the middle, then an elegant
   serif headline in 3-4 lines: cocoa words with the key words in rose italic. Make the photo with
   an image tool (not ElevenLabs) or use a photo I give you.

Writing (the same voice as the reels)
- A kind, calm older sister. One idea per post. Validate first, then one small step.
- Sound said, not written: contractions, her real-life specifics, "I know..." when it fits.
- Open with a line that feels like it was written for her ("If no one told you today...", "It's okay
  if..."); end with a gentle reminder or a soft question.
- No shame, pressure, fear, comparing women or putting down men.
- No medical, diet or money claims, no invented statistics, no fake quotes or fake screenshots of
  real people, and no verified badge unless my page is verified.

Caption (the text above the image)
- A first line that stops mid-thought so people tap "See more", then 3-6 short, warm lines, then one
  gentle question that invites comments. 0-3 hashtags.

Keep the colours, fonts and layout the same across posts so the feed looks like one brand.
```
