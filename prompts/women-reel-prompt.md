# Reusable prompt: Her Calm Corner reel (women's page)

Copy everything inside the box, fill in the three lines at the top, and send it.
In this kit, Claude loads the `her-calm-reel` skill automatically. The prompt also works on its own.

```text
Make a new reel for my women's page "Her Calm Corner" in the same structure as the approved
"If no one told you today" video.

TOPIC: [e.g. rest without guilt / a simple glow routine / saying no kindly / a small savings habit]
SCRIPT: [paste your script, or write "write it for me and show me before building"]
PALETTE: [rose (default) / lavender evening / sage morning / peach / night calm]

THE STRUCTURE (keep all of this exactly):

Format
- Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, about 28-36 seconds, built in HyperFrames (use the her-calm-reel skill).
- Faceless: no people, faces, bodies or photos. Words plus soft illustrated objects only. No emojis.

Script (50-80 words, US spelling, simple UK/US English)
- Voice of a kind, calm older sister: gentle, supportive, honest, never judging, never rushing.
- It must sound SAID, not written: contractions (you're, don't, isn't), three specifics from her real
  life ("the dishes, the emails, the texts you haven't answered"), "I know..." once, and steps with a
  size ("for a minute", "one slow breath").
- Arc: gentle opener ("If no one told you today..." / "A gentle reminder...") + the kind thing nobody
  said -> name the feeling, then her real life in specifics -> take the pressure off -> "Just do one
  small thing" + 3 tiny physical steps -> a reframe people save (2 lines) -> a kind close or gentle
  question -> CTA "Follow for more gentle reminders."
- Validate first, then one small step. No shame, pressure, fear, comparing women, putting down men,
  or medical, diet or money claims.
- Example of the register: "If no one told you today... you're doing better than you think. I know the
  list feels endless. The dishes, the emails, the texts you haven't answered. You don't have to finish
  it tonight. Just do one small thing. Fill a glass of water. Open the window for a minute. Take one
  slow breath. Rest isn't something you earn. It's part of how you keep going. So be gentle with
  yourself today. Follow for more gentle reminders."

Voice and sound
- A warm, calm female narrator that sounds like one real person talking, not a sentence reader:
  lines that belong together are spoken in one breath group, the pauses vary, soft breaths before new
  phrases, a quiet room tone, a little slower on the steps and the ending, and the longest pause after
  the breath line. ElevenLabs if available (estimate the cost first, warm and calm, not whispery or
  hyped); otherwise the local Kokoro voice in breath-group mode.
- Soft procedural sound: a bloom under the sunrise, chimes on the kindest words, air on transitions,
  wind when the overwhelm blows away, twinkles for stars, pops as cards land, a water drop, a warm pad.
  Nothing hard: no impacts, slams or sub hits.

Look
- A living pastel background under every scene: sky tints that change with the mood (dawn peach,
  heavy taupe for overwhelm, dusk lilac, warm lamp amber, sunrise), drifting colour glows, dust motes
  in the light, paper grain, a warm vignette, a thin rose progress line. No text headers.
- Cocoa text, rose italic for the comforting key words, sage for calm. Fraunces serif for statements
  (64-256 px), DM Sans for card text, the CTA and the page name.
- Soft illustrated objects with gradients, highlights and gentle shadows (not flat line art).

Story devices
- ONE orb of light travels through the whole reel and becomes each scene's object:
  sunrise -> a notification badge -> the moon -> one small glowing dot -> a drop into a glass ->
  a bubble -> the sun in a window -> the breathing circle -> the lamp glow -> the sunrise again at the end.
- Every spoken word appears on screen as it is spoken, floating up out of a soft blur. The comforting
  word writes itself in letter by letter, and the payoff gets a hand-drawn highlighter.
- Objects act on their words: cards and sticky notes pile up with the spoken items written on them,
  the moon wanes on "tonight", the glass fills on "water", the window opens on "open", the circle
  breathes on "breath", steam rises from a teacup on "rest".
- The overwhelm softens and leaves (it blows away like leaves); it never breaks or shatters.
- A different text layout in every scene: soft line + big statement, headline + words on cards,
  staircase of growing lines, centred pair over an object, text above an object, object above text in a
  beam of light, giant italic word + side lines + right-aligned payoff, final card.
- Slow camera push-ins in every scene; the small steps are one tall world the camera glides through.

Transitions (never a plain crossfade between unrelated scenes)
- Orb match cuts (the light turns into the next object), wind (the pile blows away), a warm light
  leak, camera glides (down for something falling, sideways or up for something rising), the breath
  blooming to fill the frame, and rising mist before the close.

Ending
- Hold on the kind line, the sunrise behind soft hills, "FOLLOW FOR MORE GENTLE REMINDERS" and the
  page name "HER CALM CORNER", readable as a thumbnail.

Deliver
- Show me the script first (unless I gave it). Then build, check every scene and transition in
  stills and in the rendered MP4, fix any overlaps or unreadable text, and give me a high-quality
  MP4, a share copy under 30 MB and a thumbnail, with the scene timings.
```
