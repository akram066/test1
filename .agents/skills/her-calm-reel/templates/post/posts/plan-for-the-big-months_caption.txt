Some months always cost more, and they still catch us by surprise...

I know the feeling. The heating bill, the school things, Ramadan and Eid, all landing at once.
They're not really surprises. We just don't plan for them.
Pick the big months you already know are coming.
Add up roughly what they cost, divide it by the months left, and set that small amount aside each month.
Then when they arrive, they're just months.
