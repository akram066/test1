## 1. The page

- **What it is:** a Facebook page for women about taking care of themselves: beauty, health, confidence and
  money. Name: Her Calm Corner (alternatives: Her Better Self, Soft & Strong).
- **Audience:** women aged 18–45 in the UK and the USA: students, working women, wives and mothers who feel
  tired or overwhelmed and want a calmer, better life.
- **Goal:** original content that gets shares and saves, qualifies for Facebook Content Monetization, and later
  sends followers to a digital products store.
- **Language:** simple, warm English that works in both the UK and the USA. Short sentences. US spelling.

## 2. Voice and tone (tough love, since 2026-10)

The soft "gentle reminder" reels were not getting views, so the page owner moved the voice to **tough love**:
an older sister who loves you too much to let you keep doing this. Direct, a little uncomfortable, honest,
then on your side. She **calls out the habit, never the woman**.

**Do:** open with a line that stings (a question she can't dodge, a habit named out loud). Name her real life
in specifics. Say what the habit is costing her. Turn ("Be honest...", "Here's the thing...") and give **one
small, concrete step**. Close with a challenge she can answer in the comments.

**Never:** insult her looks, body, weight, age, intelligence or worth; call her lazy, a failure or a bad
mother; mock her; compare women to each other; put down men; scare her with health or money threats.
The sting is about what she keeps doing, and the reel always ends on her side.

Example lines (the register to match):
- "How many Mondays have you promised yourself?"
- "You're not being polite. You're making yourself smaller."
- "Saving it isn't doing it."
- "You'd never let your best friend live like this. So why you?"

### Make it sound like a real person, not a poster

- **Contractions always:** you're, don't, isn't, it's, haven't.
- **Name her real life with specifics:** "the favor, the extra shift, the plans you didn't want". Three
  concrete things in a row, the last one the most personal.
- **Short punches:** 2–9 words a line. A one-line call-out lands harder than a paragraph.
- **Give the step a size she believes:** "five minutes", "one sentence", "bed before eleven".
- **Read it out loud.** If a line can't be said in one breath, split it or cut it.

## 3. Hooks

The first line is spoken at 0.0 s and is **already on screen in the first frame**: no title card, no
"A gentle reminder" lead-in. It must stop the scroll on its own. Good shapes:
- A question she can't dodge: "Count how many times you said sorry today."
- Catching her in the act: "You said yes again, didn't you?"
- A habit named out loud: "You check everyone's messages but never your bank app."
- An order: "Stop. How many of these videos have you saved?"

## 4. Writing a reel script

The voiceover drives the timing, scenes and captions, so write it first and show it to the user before
building (unless they gave the script or said go ahead).

**Length:** 65–85 words → about 30 s (keep 28–36 s). Faster than the old reels: shorter pauses, more punch.

**Arc:**

| Beat | Job | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook (1 line, 0–2 s) | the sting, on screen from frame 0 | "How many Mondays have you promised yourself?" |
| Call-out (2–3 lines) | her habit in specifics | "The new routine. The early nights. The walk. And by Wednesday, it's gone." |
| Cost (1 line) | what it's costing her | "You keep waiting to feel ready, and ready never comes." |
| Turn (1 line) | "Be honest..." / "Here's the thing..." | "Be honest. It's not Monday's fault." |
| Step (1 + up to 3 short lines) | tiny, concrete, sized | "One glass of water. Ten minutes outside. Bed before eleven." |
| Payoff (1 line) | the line people save or share | "You don't need a new week. You need one day you keep." |
| Challenge CTA (1 line) | a comment, follow or share prompt | "Which one are you starting tonight? Tell me below." |

**Rules for lines:**
- One breath per line, 2–9 words. Each line becomes a scene or a beat.
- Breath groups (`flow`) with gaps of about 0.35–0.6 s; the hook has almost no lead-in.
- Concrete nouns (phone, bank app, calendar, water, shoes) become the reel's objects.
- Key words (3–5 per reel) appear in the accent colour: the words that sting or turn.
- CTA (audience building, no product yet): a challenge question for the comments, "Follow if you're done
  [habit]", or "Send this to the friend who needs it". When a product exists, the CTA points to it.

## 5. Topic bank

Beauty: a simple glow routine (sleep, water, SPF), kindness to your reflection, slow mornings. Health habits
(no claims): rest without guilt, walks, sunlight, phone-free evenings, drinking water, stretching. Confidence:
saying no kindly, boundaries, speaking to yourself like a friend, not shrinking. Money: a small savings habit,
the "pay yourself first" jar, a calm money check-in, no-spend days, knowing your worth at work.
Overwhelm: the next small thing, done is better than perfect, asking for help, a 5-minute reset.

## 6. Safety and monetization guardrails

- Faceless always: no faces, bodies, people or photos. Objects and light carry the feeling.
- No medical, diet, weight-loss, skin-cure or money-return claims. Gentle habits, never treatments or promises.
- Sting the habit, never the person: no insults about looks, body, weight, age, worth or motherhood; no fear
  ("you're ruining your skin", "do this now or else").
- Never compare women to each other; never put down men.
- Original scripts every time; never copy another creator's lines.

## 7. Don't mix up the pages

| | Her Calm Corner (this skill) | The Strong Man Code (strong-man-reel) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice | tough-love older sister: stings, then on her side | wise older man, direct, a little cold |
| Opens with | a call-out question about her habit | a hard truth or curiosity hook |
| Device | call out the habit, then one small step | weak man vs strong man |
| Ends with | a challenge question for the comments | a rule or an order |
| Look | light paper canvas, rose and sage, serif italic | black canvas, one hard accent, condensed caps |
| Motion | floats, blooms, breathes | slams, slashes, cracks |
