## 1. The page

- **What it is:** a Facebook page for women about taking care of themselves: beauty, health, confidence and
  money. Name: Her Calm Corner (alternatives: Her Better Self, Soft & Strong).
- **Audience:** women aged 18–45 in the UK and the USA: students, working women, wives and mothers who feel
  tired or overwhelmed and want a calmer, better life.
- **Goal:** original content that gets shares and saves, qualifies for Facebook Content Monetization, and later
  sends followers to a digital products store.
- **Language:** simple, warm English that works in both the UK and the USA. Short sentences. US spelling.

## 2. Voice and tone

The page speaks like a **kind, calm older sister or close friend**: gentle, supportive and honest. She
never judges and never rushes. Every reel should leave the viewer feeling lighter and understood.

**Do:** soft, simple sentences. Speak to "you". **Validate first** ("It's okay to feel tired"), then give **one
small, practical step**. End with a kind reminder or a gentle question.

**Don't:** harsh words, shaming, pressure, fear-based claims, comparing women to each other, putting down men.

Example lines (the register to match):
- "You don't have to do everything today. Just the next small thing."
- "Rest is not a reward. It's part of the work."
- "Your glow starts with sleep, water and peace, not a 10-step routine."

## 3. Signature device: "A gentle reminder"

Many reels open with **"A gentle reminder…"** or **"If no one told you today…"**. Use it when it fits, not in
every reel, so it stays the page's recognizable voice without becoming wallpaper. Other good openers:
"It's okay if…", "This is your sign to…", "Read this slowly."

## 4. Writing a reel script

The voiceover drives the timing, scenes and captions, so write it first and show it to the user before
building (unless they gave the script or said go ahead).

**Length:** 40–70 words → 20–32 s with soft pauses. One feeling, one small step.

**Arc (the starter reel's structure):**

| Beat | Job | Example (starter reel) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opener (1 line) | the gentle device or a soft hook | "A gentle reminder." |
| Validate (1–2 lines) | name the feeling, take the pressure off | "You don't have to do everything today. Just the next small thing." |
| Small steps (2–4 very short lines) | tiny, physical, doable | "Drink a glass of water. Open the window. Breathe." |
| Reframe (1–2 lines) | the line people save | "Rest is not a reward. It's part of the work." |
| Kind close (1 line) | warmth or a gentle question | "Be kind to yourself today." |
| CTA (1 line) | soft and consistent | "Follow for more gentle reminders." |

**Rules for lines:**
- One breath per line, 2–8 words. Each line becomes a scene or a beat.
- Concrete, gentle nouns (water, window, tea, bed, journal, a walk) become the reel's objects.
- Steps must be tiny and kind: things she can do in a minute, with no cost and no judgment.
- Pauses: `short` after the opener and after each section, `long` before the reframe's payoff or the close.
- Key words (3–5 per reel) appear in rose italic on screen: the comforting words.
- CTA variants: "Follow for more gentle reminders." · "Save this for a hard day." · "Send this to a friend who needs it."
  Or a gentle question: "What's one small thing you can do for yourself today?"

## 5. Topic bank

Beauty: a simple glow routine (sleep, water, SPF), kindness to your reflection, slow mornings. Health habits
(no claims): rest without guilt, walks, sunlight, phone-free evenings, drinking water, stretching. Confidence:
saying no kindly, boundaries, speaking to yourself like a friend, not shrinking. Money: a small savings habit,
the "pay yourself first" jar, a calm money check-in, no-spend days, knowing your worth at work.
Overwhelm: the next small thing, done is better than perfect, asking for help, a 5-minute reset.

## 6. Safety and monetization guardrails

- Faceless always: no faces, bodies, people or photos. Objects and light carry the feeling.
- No medical, diet, weight-loss, skin-cure or money-return claims. Gentle habits, never treatments or promises.
- No fear, shame or urgency ("you're ruining your skin", "do this now or else").
- Never compare women to each other; never put down men.
- Original scripts every time; never copy another creator's lines.

## 7. Don't mix up the pages

| | Her Calm Corner (this skill) | The Strong Man Code (strong-man-reel) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice | kind older sister, warm, never rushes | wise older man, direct, a little cold |
| Opens with | "A gentle reminder…" | a hard truth or curiosity hook |
| Device | validate, then one small step | weak man vs strong man |
| Ends with | a kind reminder or gentle question | a rule or an order |
| Look | light paper canvas, rose and sage, serif italic | black canvas, one hard accent, condensed caps |
| Motion | floats, blooms, breathes | slams, slashes, cracks |
